Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of 11 players on an oval field around a central 22-yard pitch. The batting team tries to score runs by hitting the bowled ball and running between wickets or hitting boundaries, while the bowling and fielding team tries to dismiss batters and limit runs.

Main Formats

• Test Cricket: Played over up to five days with two innings per side and traditional white kits.
• One Day Internationals (ODIs): Played with 50 overs per side in a single day, typically in colored kits.
• Twenty20 (T20): A fast-paced format featuring 20 overs per side that usually lasts three to four hours.

Quick Cricket Lines

• Originating in South East England in the 16th century, the sport spread globally and is governed by the International Cricket Council.
• An over consists of six fair deliveries bowled from one end of the pitch.
• Hitting the ball across the boundary after it touches the ground scores four runs, while clearing the boundary on the full scores six runs.
